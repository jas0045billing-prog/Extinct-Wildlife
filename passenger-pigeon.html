Passenger Pigeon (Ectopistes migratorius)

Once the most numerous bird in North America — last died 1914 (Martha).

Quick Facts

  • Diet: Seeds, nuts, berries
  • Habitat: Eastern and central North America
  • Extinction: 1914 (last individual died in captivity)
  • Causes: Overhunting, habitat destruction
Open Gallery Back to Catalog

Story & Timeline

Passenger pigeons formed enormous flocks. Intense hunting and loss of old-growth forest nesting grounds in the 19th century caused a rapid population collapse.
