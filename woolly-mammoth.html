Woolly Mammoth (Mammuthus primigenius)

Massive Ice-Age elephant — main mainland extinction ~10,000 years ago

Quick Facts

  • Diet: Grasses, shrubs
  • Habitat: Steppe-tundra across Eurasia & North America
  • Extinction: ~10,000 years ago (mainland); small island populations persisted later
  • Causes: Climate change, human hunting
Open Gallery Back to Catalog

Story & Timeline

Woolly mammoths adapted to cold environments with long hair and fat layers. Changing climate and human pressures reduced their habitats and food, leading to extinction on the mainland.
