Dodo Raphus cucullatus

A flightless bird native to Mauritius — its loss became a symbol of human-driven extinction.

Quick Facts

  • Habitat: Mauritius islands
  • Diet: Fruits, seeds
  • Extinction: ~late 1600s
  • Causes: Hunting, introduced species, habitat loss
Open Gallery Back to Catalog

Story & Timeline

The dodo's decline accelerated after humans and non-native animals arrived; without evolved defenses, the dodo population collapsed within decades.
