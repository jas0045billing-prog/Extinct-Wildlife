About This Archive

A place to remember and learn about species that once walked the Earth.

Our Mission

The Extinction Archive was created to spread awareness about species that have disappeared due to natural causes, climate shifts, and — most tragically — human activities. By understanding their stories, we aim to inspire a deeper respect for the planet and the life it sustains.

Why This Matters

Every extinct creature represents a chapter of Earth’s fragile history. Learning about them helps us prevent the same fate from happening to endangered species today. Awareness is the first step toward conservation.

What You’ll Find Here

  • Detailed pages about extinct animals, birds, and other species
  • Image galleries with original resolution preserved
  • Lightbox views, animations, and interactive displays
  • A nature-inspired theme for an immersive experience

Sources & Credibility

All information in this archive is compiled from reliable scientific records, historical documents, paleontology research, and conservation databases.

Our Vision

To build a digital museum that is engaging, educational, visually appealing, and meaningful — allowing anyone to explore the world of lost species anytime.
